## About me
My name is Pengfei. I am 26 years old (as of 2024 August). I work as a Quantitative Developer and I write C++17 and Python 3 codes on a daily basis (among other operational duties). I am based in Singapore and I am currently a Singapore Permanent Resident (PR).

I was born and raised in China and attended Nanjing Foreign Language School. After Zhongkao (China's O-Level equivalent), I came to Singapore on a full scholarship (commonly referred to as the SM1 scholarship) and finished my high school education in Singapore. I studied Computer Science and Physics under a Double Degree Programme in National University of Singapore (NUS). I hold a First Class Honours degree in Computer Science and a Upper Second Class Honours degree in Physics.

I did three internships while I was in university, starting from a local fin-tech startup called Endowus, where I worked on an individual project under the CTO's management directly. In the next summer, I interned at Goldman as a software engineer in the Unix / Compute team. For the summer after that, I went to London and interned as a software engineer at Citadel's investor accounting team. After I graduated from university, I have been working as a quantitative developer in the financial derivatives (futures, options) market making space.

This webpage is not about careers. I don't intend it to be. It is a blog site about myself and random pieces of my thoughts. I guess I should put a disclaimer here that opinions expressed herewith are strictly mine as a private citizen and has nothing to do with anyone else or any institution.

I always liked to jot down sporadic thoughts here and there. And I like to put them together into reasonably well structured text content. (Kind of ironically I never liked humanity subjects as a student. I didn't like writing at all. I loved math and science - well, I still do. But I like writing this kind of random stuff down these days) I don't think many (any?) will come across this, or are interested in reading what I wrote. The blogs here are probably around a 10-min read each on average. I just feel like writing things down like this. And I am making public the part I feel comfortable with. I don't particularly care about the viewership (most likely 0 anyway), so I probably won't try to be politically correct (nor will I go out of my way to offend people...)

When I started out in late 2021, I thought, hey at least let me try to keep this blog site active, maybe at least 2 or 3 blogs a year? Then I got busy and had lots of other issues to deal with, and I didn't want to sac the quality here, so I kind of stopped it. But I always kinda wanted to restart this process, and as time went by it actually became harder and harder. The longer I stopped the harder it seemed to pick it back up. Now in 2024, one night, I just suddenly plucked up the courage and decided to go for it, and I am glad I did. And I rebuilt the blog site too (The first time I did this, I didn't build the blog site properly, and the blogs were piggybacking on GitHub issues. Now I think I did it much more properly this time). I don't want to put pressure on myself to publish x number of blogs each year. I mean, in the future when I have a family of the next generation maybe I will be super busy and have to put this thing down again, who knows. I don't want to make a promise just to break it.

So, please enjoy, and feel free to reach out to me via the CONTACT tab!
