'Mercer: You know what the worst days are, Olix? The days when you can't stay busy. No Krill confrontations, no spatial anomalies, just nothing going on, and all your thoughts just march inward.
Olix: So most days.' - The Orville, Season 2 Episode 1

I guess I do kind of reminisce about the old days myself too, the old days when life often had a clear structure. But now I have been reached a point where external milestones are behind me, and I am at a crossroad where it is easy to lose oneself in the vastness of possibilities going forward. I have been at this crossroad since late 2023, and I still am here.

I do try to remember the version of myself in the old days. When I was at my absolute peak, which I guess was probably comprised of largely 2 periods - one from sec 2 to sec 3 back in Nanjing Foreign Language School, the other from year 2 sem 2 to end of year 4 in National University of Singapore. So in those days, I was this absolutely driven and crazily focused person who was just very effective at what I set my mind to. I wasn't always in the best shape or form in those days, and there were definitely moments of weakness, but in general I had very clear goals in mind and I somehow could just will myself to accomplish them. Like back in NFLS, I understood I was simply not good enough to make the cut for this Singapore programme, and I practically willed myself to stay completely focused in those 2 years; In NUS, I understood there was quite a bit of gap in terms of C++ knowledge and algorithmic understanding to get into tier-1 quant firms, and I willed myself to pick all those things up and ended up with a Citadel internship. But generally speaking I just rarely have that type of fire these days. I do start to feel my age I suppose.

I mean, of course, I can still catch glimpses of that version of me every once in a while these days, but it has become increasingly difficult to maintain at that level consistently and I simply couldn't always do that. I mean, in early 2023, when I was already in survival mode, I hadn't sat for interviews for ages and my interview skillset was basically non-existent. When I finally landed some interviews here and there, I went through them purely on will. It was much easier for me back in late 2021, but I already had to really fire myself up for the interviews in early 2023. Even then, when comparing the interview quality from the ones I did in late 2021, it was still quite far from my best but it just happened to be enough. Now, back to today, I think I just rarely have that type of fire and I think I have fallen off a bit from my absolute peak. I am not sure if I could ever get back to the state of consistently being extremely focused and spending as much energy as I possibly can.

This is a relatively short blog. I have already briefly touched upon the emptiness and the mix of feelings ever since I graduated in the previous blog and in this blog, I discussed the difference in my state and my form. I think in the next blog, I should finally (well, hopefully) be ready to elaborate a bit more on my current job and how it has been for me 1.5 years into it.
